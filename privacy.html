Privacy policy

Last updated October 16, 2026

Prereq uses your account and coursework information to help you build and save an academic plan.

Information we collect

Prereq stores your account identifier, sign-in email address, profile name when provided, and connected sign-in method. It also stores information you enter about your school, grade, graduation year, completed and planned courses, study halls, summer coursework, prior learning, and placement confirmations.

If you send feedback, your message, account ID, app version, and contact preference are included. Please avoid including passwords or sensitive information in feedback.

How we use information

We use this information to sign you in, load your school’s catalog, save your coursework and plan, check course requirements, suggest pathways, calculate projected graduation progress, respond to feedback, and provide account support.

Prereq does not sell personal information, serve advertising, or track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

Services that help run Prereq

Supabase provides authentication and storage for account-linked app data. Apple or Google handles sign-in, depending on the method you choose. Your sign-in provider supplies an authentication identifier and any email address or name you permit it to share. Apple may supply a private relay email address.

Academic plans and course history are not sent to Apple or Google as part of sign-in. These providers handle information under their own privacy policies. You can read the policies for Supabase, Apple, and Google.

Storage and security

Account-linked information is stored through Supabase. The app also keeps local data on your device. Access controls are used to restrict access to your account records. No method of storage or transmission can guarantee complete security.

Keeping and deleting information

Your account information is kept to provide the service while you use Prereq. You can edit your coursework and plan in the app.

To delete your account, open Profile → Account settings → Delete account. After confirmation and reauthentication, deletion removes your account and associated active application records, along with profile-specific local app data. Service providers may retain operational logs or backups according to their retention practices.

If you cannot access your account or have a question about retained information, email prereq.contact@gmail.com.

This website

These pages contain no advertising, analytics scripts, or sign-in forms. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process technical information, such as your IP address, to deliver and secure it. See GitHub’s privacy statement. Opening an external link takes you to a service with its own privacy practices.

Changes to this policy

Updates will appear on this page with a revised date. Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Prereq at prereq.contact@gmail.com.
