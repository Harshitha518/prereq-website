Plan your courses
with Prereq.

A simple way to explore courses, understand prerequisites, and organize your high school plan.

Start with the courses you’ve completed. Then explore what you can take next and build a plan that fits your interests.

Keep the whole picture in view.

Browse your school’s catalog, compare course requirements, and see how your choices fit together across four years. Suggested pathways help you explore possible routes, while credit summaries help you review your progress.

Your plan is a starting point for a conversation with your counselor. Your school makes the final decisions about course placement and graduation.

Need a hand?

For questions, feedback, or a course that doesn’t look right, email prereq.contact@gmail.com.
