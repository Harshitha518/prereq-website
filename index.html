The app

Start with your
completed courses.

Select your school and grade, then enter your completed coursework, including courses taken before high school. Prereq uses that history when you add courses and compare pathways.

01Catalog

What would I need to take first?

Some courses have several ways to qualify. Prereq groups the conditions for each option and shows which courses you need beforehand or can take in the same year.

02Add courses

What can I take next?

See courses whose prerequisites you meet, or browse the full list for your grade. Each entry shows its requirements, including any teacher recommendation or department approval.

03Pathways

What are my options?

Compare course sequences in a subject, along with any required coursework in other subjects. Review the supporting options, then add your chosen pathway and its supporting courses to your schedule.

04Plan

What if I change my mind?

Drag courses between years and semesters as your plans change. Prereq checks for conflicts. Progress shows which graduation requirements you’ve completed, which your plan covers, and what you still need.

Counselor conversations

Review your plan
with your counselor.

Export your schedule, graduation progress, and review items as a PDF. Bring it to your counselor to discuss your choices, ask questions, and work through any requirements that need school confirmation.

Behind the app

Hi, I'm Harshitha! 👋

Back in eighth grade, I spent way too many evenings trying to figure out my four-year high school course plan. Between course catalogs, prerequisites, and graduation requirements, I struggled to make sense of how everything fit together. I even ended up with a five-page document trying to organize it all!

Once I got to high school, I realized it didn't get much easier. My interests kept changing, and figuring out how one course could affect my options years later meant going through everything all over again.

That's why I started building Prereq! What began as a small prototype grew into an app I've put a lot of thought into, especially when it comes to making course planning feel simple, flexible, and actually enjoyable.

There's still so much I'd love to add, but I hope Prereq makes planning your high school courses a little easier and a lot less overwhelming than it was for me :)

Harshitha
