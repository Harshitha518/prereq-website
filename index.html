The app

Check requirements.
Plan the years ahead.

Add completed courses, including middle school coursework. Use that record while exploring classes and planning grades 9-12.

01Catalog

Read more than the course title.

Browse by subject, then check what a class covers, its credits, eligible grades, and prerequisite options. Minimum course grades and school recommendations appear with the requirements.

02Add courses

Find courses whose prerequisites are met.

Use Prereqs met to see courses that fit the coursework already entered. Switch to the grade’s course list to explore other choices and see what is still required.

03Pathways

Work backward from a course.

Choose a course to explore possible sequences leading up to it. See which classes would need to come first or be taken together, based on completed coursework.

04Plan

Check all four years together.

Place courses by grade and semester, then review prerequisite conflicts and annual credits. Graduation progress combines completed and planned courses to show which requirements still need attention.

Review with a counselor

Take a draft schedule
to a counselor.

Export the plan as a PDF to discuss course choices, recommendations, and placement approvals. Update it in Prereq after the meeting. The school makes final placement and graduation decisions.
